Section II: Triangle Meshes and Half-Edge Data Structure

Part 3: Area-weighted vertex normals

To compute the area-weighted normal at a vertex, I traversed all incident triangles using the half-edge structure. For each non-boundary triangle, I computed the cross product of two of its edge vectors and added the result to an accumulated normal. Because the magnitude of this cross product is twice the triangle's area, larger triangles naturally contribute more weight. Finally, I normalized the accumulated vector to produce a unit vertex normal.
